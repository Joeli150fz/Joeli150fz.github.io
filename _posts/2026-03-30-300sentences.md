## Part 2: Normal Sentences
I'm planning to visit my parents this weekend.  
Have you finished your homework yet?  
I've been working on this project for weeks.  
She always arrives on time for meetings.  
I'm thinking about changing my job.  
Could you please pass me the salt?  
I appreciate everything you've done for me.  
(Explanation: Appreciate means to be thankful for something, to recognize the value of someone's help or kindness.)  
The deadline is coming up really fast.  
(Explanation: Deadline means the last date or time by which you must finish something.)  
I need to prepare for tomorrow's presentation.  
He's been studying English for 3 years.  
I'm getting used to the new system.  
(Explanation: Getting used to means becoming comfortable with something new, adapting to a new situation.)  
That restaurant is worth trying out.  
(Explanation: Worth trying out means it's good enough that you should try it. It deserves your time.)  
I've never been to that part of town.  
We should probably leave before rush hour.  
(Explanation: Rush hour is the time of day when traffic is heaviest, usually when people go to work or come home.)  
I'm trying to save money for vacation.  
She reminds me of someone I used to know.  
(Explanation: Reminds me of means she is similar to someone. She makes me think of another person.)  
I can't seem to figure this out.  
(Explanation: Figure this out means to understand something, to solve a problem or puzzle.)  
Would you mind closing the window?  
I'm looking forward to the weekend.  
They've decided to postpone the meeting.  
I haven't heard from him in ages.  
This situation is getting complicated.  
I'm supposed to pick up my kids at 5.  
The instructions are a bit confusing.  
I recommend checking the weather forecast.  
She's always complaining about something.  
I'm considering taking a course online.  
We had to cancel our plans last minute.  
I'm not comfortable with that idea.  
He tends to exaggerate sometimes.  

##  Part 3: Difficult Sentences
I have been thinking about this problem for many weeks.  
(Explanation: Thinking about means trying to understand a problem. Problem means a difficult situation.)  
The company's money is going up and down.  
(Explanation: Going up and down means it keeps changing. Money means income.)  
I am very tired because I had many meetings.  
(Explanation: Very tired means exhausted.)  
She can explain difficult things in an easy way.  
(Explanation: Explain means tell clearly. Easy way means simple words.)  
This project will succeed only if we get enough money.  
(Explanation: Succeed means be successful. Enough money means required funds.)  
I am trying to improve my work habits.  
(Explanation: Improve means make better. Work habits means daily work routine.)  
His idea sounded good but it was not perfect.  
(Explanation: Sounded good means looked right. Not perfect means had problems.)  
This decision will affect many people.  
(Explanation: Affect means change something. Many people means wide impact.)  
I am not sure if this plan will work.  
(Explanation: Not sure means having doubts. Work means be successful.)  
She stays calm even in stressful situations.  
(Explanation: Calm means relaxed. Stressful situations means pressure.)  
This new situation needs new ideas.  
I am thinking about studying in another country.  
The discussion has reached an important moment.  
(Explanation: Important moment means decision time.)  
His behavior shows he is hiding something.
(Explanation: Behavior means actions. Hiding means not telling.)
I am trying to balance work and personal life.  
(Explanation: Balance means giving time to both sides.)
The data supports our first idea.  
(Explanation: Supports means proves.)  
She is famous for being very careful.  
The new rules will harm weak people.  
(Explanation: Harm means cause problems.)  
I support a clear and honest decision process.  
He has very strong knowledge in this field.  
The company is making big changes.  
I believe his statement is true.  
This situation is more difficult than we thought.  
She has natural leadership skills.  
The evidence proves what people said.  
I am worried about the result of this investment.  
His thinking is practical and simple.  
The group is talking about difficult issues.  
I am trying to handle many tasks at once.  
(Explanation: Handle means manage.)  
This plan does not have enough proof.  
She is good at leading people.  
I feel stressed because of my workload.  
This decision can change our future.  
I need more time to think about this.  
He is responsible for this project.  
(Explanation: Responsible means in charge.)  
She speaks clearly in meetings.  
We must find a better solution.  
This task is harder than it looks.  
I am learning from my mistakes.  
He agreed after some discussion.  
She works well under pressure.  
This plan needs small changes.  
I will take full responsibility.  
The results are better than before.  
We should focus on the main goal.  
He explained the problem simply.
